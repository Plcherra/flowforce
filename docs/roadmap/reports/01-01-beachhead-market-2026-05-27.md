# Phase 01.01 - Beachhead Market

Date: 2026-05-27

## Decision

FlowForce starts with independent and small multi-location restaurants/food-service operators.

This is the best first market because the current product direction already combines the pain points that restaurant operators usually split across multiple tools:

- Staff scheduling and labor planning.
- Inventory counts, purchasing, suppliers, waste, and production.
- Forms, checklists, SOPs, inspections, and manager review.
- Team messaging, tasks, announcements, and approvals.
- Owner dashboards, cost visibility, and AI recommendations.

## Buyer

Primary buyer:

- Owner/operator of an independent restaurant or small restaurant group.

Secondary buyers:

- General manager.
- District manager.
- Franchise operator.
- Operations director.

## Daily Users

- Owner/operator: uses FlowForce to understand cost, risk, execution quality, and business health.
- General manager: uses FlowForce to run schedule, labor, inventory, tasks, and daily execution.
- Shift lead: uses FlowForce to complete checklists, manage tasks, report issues, and handle shift-level exceptions.
- Staff: uses FlowForce for schedule, messages, tasks, training, forms, and simple self-service.
- Admin/bookkeeper: uses FlowForce for setup, expenses, purchasing, payroll/accounting exports, and permissions.

## Top 5 Painful Workflows

1. Scheduling and labor planning live away from inventory, prep, and demand.
2. Inventory counts, purchasing, waste, and production live away from daily staff execution.
3. Opening, closing, cleaning, food safety, and checklist compliance live away from tasks and manager review.
4. Team communication lives away from task assignment, approvals, schedule changes, and announcements.
5. Owner reporting is manually stitched together from scheduling, inventory, payroll/accounting, POS exports, and spreadsheets.

## Product Implication

The first version of FlowForce should not try to be a generic workforce tool. It should feel like a restaurant operations command center:

- Schedule staff.
- Know what inventory and prep the schedule requires.
- Run opening/closing and compliance workflows.
- Track waste and purchasing.
- Show labor + inventory + operating cost in one dashboard.
- Let AI explain risks and suggest manager actions with evidence.

## Acceptance Result

- Primary vertical selected: restaurant/food-service.
- Buyer and daily user personas selected.
- First five workflows selected.
- Product is no longer positioned as a generic app for every business type.
