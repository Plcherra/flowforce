# Phase 01.02 - Replacement Stack

Date: 2026-05-27

## Decision

FlowForce should be positioned as a restaurant operations platform that can replace parts of the existing checklist, workforce, and inventory stack over time, but v1 should make careful claims.

The sales story is not "we have every feature they have." The sales story is:

> FlowForce connects daily restaurant execution, staff scheduling, inventory, purchasing, waste, and cost visibility in one workspace.

## Competitor Reference Points

Dedicated restaurant checklist platforms commonly emphasize daily checklists, food-safety workflows, temperature monitoring/sensors, labeling, employee scheduling, accountability, and performance tools.

Frontline workforce tools commonly include time clock, job scheduling, forms, quick tasks, employee reminders, live communication around submissions, and payroll export/integration workflows.

MarketMan's official platform messaging emphasizes restaurant inventory, purchase orders, POS/distributor/accounting integrations, real-time inventory tracking, manual mobile counts, menu profitability, actual versus theoretical reporting, COGS, and gross profit reports.

Sources checked:

- MarketMan restaurant inventory software: https://www.marketman.com/platform/restaurant-inventory-management-software

## What FlowForce Replaces From Dedicated Checklist Platforms

V1 replacement target:

- Operations checklists and SOPs.
- Opening/closing workflows.
- Food-safety-style forms and compliance records.
- Corrective tasks from failed checks.
- Manager review queues.
- Execution reporting by location, person, and workflow.

Do not claim yet:

- Remote temperature sensor hardware parity.
- Label-printer parity.
- Mature food-safety hardware ecosystem parity.

## What FlowForce Replaces From Frontline Workforce Tools

V1 replacement target:

- Employee directory and roles.
- Scheduling and availability basics.
- Tasks and reminders.
- Company updates and team communication.
- Forms/checklists.
- Basic training/learning, recognition, and permissions.
- Manager/admin execution visibility.

Do not claim yet:

- Full time-clock/payroll parity.
- Mature GPS/geofencing parity.
- Broad payroll provider integration parity.
- Every HR compliance workflow.

## What FlowForce Replaces From MarketMan

V1 replacement target:

- Inventory items, units, categories, locations, and suppliers.
- Counts and stock position.
- Purchasing and purchase lines.
- Recipes/production/prep logic.
- Waste and adjustments.
- Food-cost and COGS-style visibility.
- Inventory-linked owner dashboards.

Do not claim yet:

- Full distributor catalog/order integration parity.
- Full POS integration parity.
- Full accounting integration parity.
- Mature actual-versus-theoretical reporting until sales/POS and recipe data are connected.

## FlowForce's Honest Advantage

FlowForce should win by connecting the things competitors often separate:

- Schedule labor and understand the inventory/prep impact.
- Track inventory and understand the staffing/operational impact.
- Run checklists and automatically create tasks or reviews.
- Show owner dashboards that combine labor, inventory, waste, purchasing, and execution.
- Let AI explain risks and suggest manager actions with evidence.

## Acceptance Result

- Dedicated checklist platform replacement target documented.
- Frontline workforce replacement target documented.
- MarketMan replacement target documented.
- V1 non-claims documented.
- The replacement story is honest and specific.
