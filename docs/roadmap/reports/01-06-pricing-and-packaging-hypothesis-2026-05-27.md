# Phase 01.06 - Pricing And Packaging Hypothesis

Date: 2026-05-27

## Decision

FlowForce should use a location-based pricing model with employee bands.

Restaurants often have fluctuating staff counts, seasonal workers, part-time employees, and turnover. Pure per-seat pricing can make adoption feel punitive. A location-first package is easier for owners to understand and better aligned with the restaurant operating model.

This is a hypothesis for product planning, not final published pricing.

## Packaging Model

### Starter

Purpose:

- Low-friction entry for one restaurant location that needs team coordination and daily execution.

Likely includes:

- Company setup.
- Employee directory.
- Roles and permissions basics.
- Scheduling basics.
- Tasks and reminders.
- Company updates and team communication basics.
- Simple forms/checklists.
- Basic dashboard.

### Operations

Purpose:

- Primary paid pilot package and the likely core package for serious restaurant operators.

Likely includes:

- Everything in Starter.
- Inventory setup.
- Inventory counts.
- Suppliers and locations.
- Purchasing basics.
- Waste tracking.
- Recipes/production basics.
- Opening/closing/food-safety checklist templates.
- Manager review queues.
- Operational reports.

### Pro

Purpose:

- Premium package for operators who want FlowForce's main differentiator: connected cost and execution intelligence.

Likely includes:

- Everything in Operations.
- Inventory + scheduling + cost engine.
- Shift profitability preview.
- Prep and purchasing planner.
- Waste impact board.
- Execution quality board.
- AI recommendations with evidence and manager approval.
- Advanced reports.
- Integrations and migration support.
- Multi-location controls.

## Core Versus Premium

Core:

- Onboarding, company setup, profiles, employees, roles, settings.
- Scheduling basics.
- Tasks.
- Messages/company updates.
- Forms/checklists.
- Inventory setup and counts.
- Basic purchasing/waste.
- Basic dashboard and reports.

Premium:

- Cross-module cost engine.
- AI recommendations.
- Advanced dashboards.
- Integration ecosystem.
- Migration tooling.
- Multi-location analytics and controls.

## Pricing Direction

Use:

- Base price per location.
- Employee bands to prevent abuse and support scaling.
- Optional add-ons for heavy integration, migration, and advanced AI usage.

Avoid for v1:

- Pure per-seat pricing.
- Overly complex module-by-module pricing.
- Enterprise custom pricing before pilot learning.

## Expected Savings Story

FlowForce should not only promise lower software bills. The stronger savings story is operational:

- Fewer disconnected apps.
- Fewer spreadsheets.
- Less manual reconciliation between schedule, inventory, tasks, and reports.
- Better labor planning.
- Less preventable waste.
- Faster manager follow-through.
- Clearer owner visibility.

The software replacement story remains useful:

- Jolt-style operations.
- Connecteam-style workforce coordination.
- MarketMan-style inventory/cost basics.

But the premium value is:

> One operating picture instead of three separate systems.

## Acceptance Result

- Starter, Operations, and Pro package concepts defined.
- Core and premium modules separated.
- Location-based pricing with employee bands selected as the working model.
- Savings story defined around both software consolidation and operational margin.
