# Phase 01.03 - Core Product Promise

Date: 2026-05-27

## Decision

FlowForce's core promise should be concrete, restaurant-specific, and centered on connected operations rather than feature volume.

The product should not sound like a generic "all-in-one business app." It should sound like a restaurant operating system that ties daily execution to cost and visibility.

## Core Positioning Statement

> FlowForce is the restaurant operations command center that connects staff scheduling, daily execution, inventory, purchasing, waste, cost visibility, and AI recommendations in one workspace.

## Owner-Facing ROI Statement

> Replace disconnected workforce, checklist, and inventory tools with one system that shows what each shift costs, where waste is happening, what needs attention today, and how to protect margin before small problems become expensive.

Why this works:

- It speaks to margin and control.
- It explains why the connected system matters.
- It avoids overclaiming full parity with every competitor module.

## Manager-Facing Workflow Statement

> Build the schedule, run opening and closing, assign tasks, track counts and waste, review exceptions, and see the day's risks from one manager dashboard instead of jumping between apps and spreadsheets.

Why this works:

- It describes a manager's day.
- It connects scheduling, execution, inventory, and review.
- It makes the app feel operational, not abstract.

## Staff-Facing Simplicity Statement

> Staff get one place to see their schedule, messages, tasks, checklists, training, and shift updates, with fewer logins and clearer expectations.

Why this works:

- It emphasizes clarity and simplicity.
- It avoids manager/owner-heavy language for staff.
- It supports mobile-first field execution.

## Short UI Version

> One connected command center for restaurant labor, inventory, execution, and cost.

## Homepage Copy Direction

Headline:

> Run the shift. Control the cost. See the whole operation.

Supporting copy:

> FlowForce brings scheduling, tasks, checklists, inventory, purchasing, waste, and AI-powered operations insight into one workspace for restaurant teams.

## Product Implications

This promise means product decisions should prioritize:

- Connected dashboard visibility over isolated module pages.
- Inventory + scheduling + labor/cost connections.
- Manager daily workflows.
- Staff mobile simplicity.
- AI recommendations that cite operational evidence.

This promise also means product decisions should deprioritize:

- Generic business templates before restaurant workflows are strong.
- Mature competitor parity claims that are not built yet.
- Large monorepo/native rewrites before core workflows are shippable.

## Acceptance Result

- Short positioning statement created.
- Owner-facing ROI statement created.
- Manager-facing workflow statement created.
- Staff-facing simplicity statement created.
- Reusable homepage/dashboard copy created.
